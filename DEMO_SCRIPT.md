# VERIFYX — Submission Demo Video Script

**Total Target Duration**: 60 – 90 Seconds  
**Format**: Screen capture walkthrough with voiceover overlay  

---

### [0:00 – 0:05] INTRO & TITLE
- **Visual**: Show VERIFYX home screen with animated dark-mode interface and pulsing neon badge.
- **Voiceover**: *"This is VERIFYX — a phone-first, offline-first AI physical-world verification engine."*

### [0:05 – 0:15] THE PROBLEM
- **Visual**: Brief visual transition showing traditional paper/PDF safety checklist.
- **Voiceover**: *"Workplace safety standards exist digitally, but physical verification remains manual, slow, and subjective. Inspectors inspect, photograph, and report manually — leaving physical evidence unverified."*

### [0:15 – 0:30] POINT & SCAN (LOCAL AI INFERENCE)
- **Visual**: Click **START VERIFICATION**, select **Dataset Demo / Evaluation Mode**, click **Fire Extinguisher**. Show live ONNX bounding box overlays, latency HUD (~500ms WASM), and confidence metrics (94%).
- **Voiceover**: *"VERIFYX turns your device into an autonomous verification tool. Running local YOLO11n AI inside the browser via WebAssembly, it detects safety assets instantly — without cloud servers or privacy risks."*

### [0:30 – 0:45] VERIFY & ISSUE (SPATIAL RULE ENGINE)
- **Visual**: Switch scenario to **Clear Pathway**. Show chair bounding box intersecting walking corridor. Red box appears with label **ISSUE DETECTED (Corridor Obstructed)**. Click **VIEW RESULTS** and **ISSUE DETAILS**.
- **Voiceover**: *"Perception is not verification. VERIFYX passes detections through a spatial rule engine. Here, a chair encroaches on the primary egress corridor. VERIFYX flags the hazard and calculates spatial overlap."*

### [0:45 – 0:60] FIX & RE-SCAN (CLOSED-LOOP VERIFICATION)
- **Visual**: Click **FIX & RESCAN**. Show re-scan view confirming pathway clearance. Click **COMPLETE RESCAN**.
- **Voiceover**: *"Crucially, an issue is never marked resolved without new visual proof. Following the 'Point, Verify, Fix, Verify Again' workflow, the inspector clears the physical hazard and re-scans to confirm resolution."*

### [0:60 – 0:75] VERIFIED & AUDIT HISTORY
- **Visual**: Screen transitions to **VERIFIED (ALL CHECKS SATISFIED)** with green badge. Show initial issue photo and cleared re-scan frame displayed side-by-side.
- **Voiceover**: *"With new evidence captured, the check transitions to verified. VERIFYX preserves the original violation photo and cleared re-scan frame side-by-side in immutable session history."*

### [0:75 – 0:90] INSPECTION REPORT & OUTRO
- **Visual**: Click **VIEW REPORT**. Scroll through professional compliance document showing session metrics, check table, timestamps, and **EXPORT JSON / PRINT** options.
- **Voiceover**: *"Finally, VERIFYX generates a complete, print-ready compliance audit report — entirely offline. VERIFYX: Point. Verify. Fix. Verify Again."*
